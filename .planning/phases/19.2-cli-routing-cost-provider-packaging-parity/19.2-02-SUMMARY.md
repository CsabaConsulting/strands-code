# Phase 19.2 Plan 02: Session Toggles, Diff Previews, Session Prefixes & `/skills` Parity Summary

**Unified all 5 REPL toggles as session-sticky in-memory state backed by `config.yaml` startup defaults, added bounded sanitized `unified_diff` previews to HITL `write`/`edit` prompts and `/diff show [path]`, scoped `PendingStore` per session id, unified session-prefix matching across `/resume`, `/forget`, and `--session-id`, and pruned legacy CLI/alias flags.**

## Accomplishments
- **Session-sticky toggles & `config.yaml` consolidation (`D-01`):** Removed `strands_code_cli/policy/diff_config.py`, `MemoryModeConfig`, `codeact_remember`, and `save_codeact_choice()`. Consolidated startup defaults (`codeact`, `diff_mode`, `memory_mode`) in `ProviderConfig` (`strands_code_cli/entry/provider_config.py`) and introduced `DiffModeState` in `strands_code_cli/policy/diff_gate.py` so `/diff`, `/memory mode`, `/codeact`, `/mode`, and `/route` all stay in-memory per session.
- **Safe bounded `unified_diff` & session-scoped `PendingStore` (`D-02`):** Retired `/diff approve-each` and `_default_ask`; scoped `PendingStore` to `pending-<session_id>.json` when `session_id` is supplied; fixed `/diff SHOW` case normalization and rendered bounded sanitized `unified_diff` previews in `/diff show [path]` and `PolicyGateHandler._render_prompt_header` (zero-I/O `preview_for_edit` for `edit`, confined/non-sensitive/symlink-refused/regular/`<=256KiB`/strict-UTF-8 disk read for `write`).
- **Unified session prefixes, explicit `--codeact`, & pruned aliases (`D-03`):** Added `session_index.resolve_session_prefix` and wired it into `/resume`, `/forget`, and `--session-id` (`resolve_session_id` with test-double safety). Retired `_expand_bare_codeact`, `/models`, `/skill`, `/skills refresh`, `/skills uninstall`; fixed bare `/skills marketplace|show|remove`; rejected unknown `--flag` tokens in `_parse_flag_args`.
