# Phase 19.2 Plan 03: Sole `engine.order` Schema, 5-Engine `/route setup`, `_route_pre_turn` Fix & `/cost` Parity Summary

**Retired legacy `engine.primary` across routing and sandbox detection, wired all 5 engines cleanly into `/route setup` with active-session auto-enable, stopped double-recording/phantom prints on failed routed switches, preserved paid tokens/cost across held/`Uncertain` asks, and aligned `/cost` lanes and `/cost refresh` output.**

## Accomplishments
- **Sole `engine.order` schema & empty-order disable (`D-04`):** Removed `primary`, `_VALID_PRIMARY_ENGINES`, `_LEGACY_PRIMARY_ORDER`, and `_validate_engine_legacy_primary` from `strands_code_cli/routing/core.py` and `strands_code_cli/sandbox/box.py`. Updated `docs/ROUTING.md` and `chain_skip_notes()` (adds `"openrouter engine skipped: OPENROUTER_API_KEY not set"`).
- **5-engine `/route setup` & `RoutingState` auto-enable (`D-04`):** Expanded `_ENGINE_LABELS` and `_setup_engine_order()` to auto-detect `native` + `decisions_api` while exposing all 5 engines (`native`, `decisions_api`, `openrouter`, `local`, `llm`). Prompted for `decisions_api_model` and `llm_model` via dict-driven `_setup_gather_engine_models` / `_setup_finalize` and auto-enabled `RoutingState` on strategy save.
- **Pre-turn double-record fix & `/cost` parity (`D-04`, `D-05`):** Updated `_apply_routed_pick` to return `(model, applied)` so `_route_pre_turn` skips `_record_routing_event` and `_print_routed_note` when held. Accumulated paid tokens/spend across `_decide_effort`, `_resolve_effort`, `_evaluate_model_answer`, `_run_chain`, and missing-question `invalid body` in `transports.py`. Updated `cost_context.py` so `native` & `llm` (including resolve-held events via `decision_engine`) classify into estimated spend, `decisions_api` / paid-unpriced rows render `cost n/a`, and `refresh_report()` renders `Decisions:`.
