# Phase 19.2 Plan 04: OpenAI & `bedrock-mantle/` Effort Guards and `validate_routed_effort()` Summary

**Unified per-model OpenAI reasoning/effort guards across `openai/` and `bedrock-mantle/openai.*` (`#6522` / `PR #6525` bridge) and aligned `validate_routed_effort()` with OpenAI, Mantle, and upstream `_MODEL_LEVELS` capabilities.**

## Accomplishments
- **Unified OpenAI & Mantle capability table (`D-07`):** Added `_openai_family_tail()`, `_has_dash_prefix()`, `_openai_effort_spec()`, and `openai_supported_efforts()` in `strands_code_cli/model/model_switch.py`. Covers `o1`/`o3`/`o4-mini`, `gpt-5`/`gpt-5-mini`/`gpt-5-nano`, `gpt-5.1`/`5.2`/`5.4`/`5.5`, `-pro`, `-codex`, `gpt-oss-*`, and `gpt-6-astra`/`6.1-sol`/`6.2-*` across both `openai/` and `bedrock-mantle/openai.*` while leaving `bedrock-mantle/google.gemma-4-*` untouched.
- **Token-boundary non-reasoning guard (`D-07`):** Updated `_is_openai_non_reasoning()` and `supports_reasoning()` to match `o1-mini*`, `o1-preview*`, `gpt-4.5*`, `gpt-4o*`, `gpt-4.1*`, `gpt-4-turbo*`, `gpt-4-*`, `gpt-4`, `chatgpt-*`, `gpt-3.5*`, and `*chat-latest*` without false-matching `o1`, `o10`, `gpt-5`, or `gpt-4.10`. Updated `configure_openai_for_model()` and `_prepare_harness_effort()` (tagged `# Upstream: strands-agents/harness-sdk#6522 (PR #6525) — retire openai/ portion once released`) to remove the blanket `_bedrock_mantle_config` skip while preserving non-OpenAI Mantle models.
- **Routed effort pre-mutation alignment (`D-07`):** Added `_effort_supported_by_target()` to `strands_code_cli/routing/core.py:validate_routed_effort()`, checking `supports_reasoning()`, `openai_supported_efforts()`, and upstream `strands_harness.models._MODEL_LEVELS` (such as `bedrock-mantle/google.gemma-4-*` allowing only `none`/`high` plus `auto`/`off`) so mismatches hold cleanly without coercing `Pick.effort`.
- **Test coverage:** Updated existing `test_model_switch.py` assertions and added `test_configure_openai_gpt51_through_62_strips_and_clamps`, `test_openai_and_bedrock_mantle_non_reasoning_boundaries`, `test_openai_and_bedrock_mantle_reasoning_matrix`, and `test_validate_holds_unsupported_openai_mantle_and_gemma_efforts`.
